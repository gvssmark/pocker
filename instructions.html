Family Hold'em

How to Play

A quick guide for anyone joining a game night — no poker experience assumed.

Joining a table

Whoever's hosting will send you a link and a room code (4 letters, like RJ4K) — usually over WhatsApp.

  1. Open the link.
  2. Type your name (and optionally tap the circle to add a photo).
  3. Enter the room code under "Join a table" and tap Join table.

That's it — no account, no password. You'll land at the table and see everyone else already seated.

Playing your hand

Your two cards always sit in a strip at the bottom of your own screen — nobody else can see them. Everyone else's cards show as face-down backs on the table.

When it's your turn, you'll see buttons for Fold, Check or Call, and Bet/Raise (drag the slider to choose an amount, then Confirm). When it's not your turn, you'll just see whose turn it is.

You have 2 minutes to act, with a countdown shown on screen. If time runs out, the app checks for you if that's free, or folds you if there's a bet to call — so one person stepping away doesn't stall everyone else.

Taking a break

Need to step away mid-game? Tap the ☰ menu in the top-left → Take a break. The host approves it, and you'll sit out (folded from any hand already in progress) until you come back — tap Return to the table in the same menu whenever you're ready.

Joining a game already in progress

You can join with the room code any time, even mid-game. You'll be seated right away, but you'll wait out whatever hand is currently being played — you're dealt in automatically starting with the very next hand.

A few things worth knowing

  • If you close the app or lose signal, just reopen the same link — you'll drop right back into your seat.
  • The host's device needs to stay open for the game to keep running — they're the one dealing the cards behind the scenes.
  • Chip standings are always available from the menu if you want to check who's up.
  • On an iPhone, you can add the game to your home screen for quicker access: open the link in Safari, tap the Share icon, then "Add to Home Screen."
